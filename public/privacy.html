Privacy, in plain language.

StillDue stores the information needed to keep your deadlines available across your devices. It does not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Information StillDue stores

  • Your email address, display name, and authentication identity.
  • The deadlines, boards, projects, templates, notes, and preferences you create.
  • Basic technical logs used to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Demo work stays in your browser. Signed-in work is stored in StillDue’s Supabase project and protected so each account can access only its own workspace.

Google sign-in

When you choose Google sign-in, StillDue requests only the basic identity information needed to create your account: your name, email address, profile picture, and Google account identifier. StillDue does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, or your contacts.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate you, synchronize your workspace, apply your display preferences, maintain security, and provide features you request. It is not used to build advertising profiles.

Service providers

StillDue uses Supabase for authentication and database storage, Netlify for hosting and server functions, and Google when you choose Google sign-in. These providers process data to deliver their part of the service.

Your choices

You can export your workspace from Settings. You can also delete your account there; account deletion removes the workspace data associated with your StillDue account. You may revoke StillDue’s Google access from your Google Account settings.
